What Is Edge Support in a Mattress?

So, what is edge support in a mattress?

Edge support refers to how well the perimeter of a mattress resists compression when weight is placed near its sides. Mattress manufacturers accomplish this in several ways depending on the type of mattress.

A hybrid mattress may use high-density foam around the perimeter of its coil system. A foam mattress may use firmer or denser support foam near the sides, while an adjustable air mattress can incorporate reinforced perimeter materials around its air chamber system.

Regardless of construction, the goal is similar: create a more stable mattress from edge to edge.

Why Does Mattress Edge Support Matter?

Strong mattress edge support can affect how a mattress feels in several everyday situations.

One of the biggest benefits is usable sleeping space. If the outer few inches of a mattress collapse excessively, sleepers naturally move toward the center. A stronger perimeter allows you to comfortably use more of the mattress.

Edge support can also matter when sitting on the side of the bed. Many people sit on their mattress while getting dressed, putting on shoes or simply getting in and out of bed.

For couples sharing a queen or king mattress, stronger edges can be particularly useful because both sleepers can make better use of the full width of the bed.

Which Mattress Type Has the Best Edge Support?

There isn't one construction that automatically provides the best mattress edge support. The actual design of the mattress matters more than simply whether it's foam, hybrid, air or innerspring.

However, there are some general differences.

Hybrid mattresses can provide excellent edge stability when the coil system is surrounded by reinforced high-density foam.

Memory foam mattresses can provide good edge support when they incorporate dense support foams designed to resist excessive compression.

Adjustable air mattresses can also offer strong edge support when the air chambers are surrounded by a reinforced perimeter system.

This is why it's worth looking beyond the mattress category and examining how the individual mattress is actually constructed.

What Should You Look for in a Mattress with Edge Support?

When comparing mattresses, don't simply look for the words "edge support." Consider how the manufacturer creates that support.

Look for reinforced foam perimeters, high-density support foams, reinforced coil systems or dedicated edge-support technologies.

You should also consider the rest of the mattress. Excellent edge support won't compensate for a mattress that's too firm, too soft or otherwise uncomfortable for you.

The goal is to find the right combination of edge support, comfort, firmness and overall mattress construction.

Is a Firm Mattress Better for Edge Support?

Not always.

A firm mattress can feel more stable around the perimeter simply because the entire sleeping surface compresses less. But firmness and edge support aren't the same thing.

A medium mattress with a properly reinforced perimeter may have better edge support than a firm mattress without any dedicated edge reinforcement.

This is another reason to pay attention to construction rather than judging edge support solely by the mattress's firmness rating.

Is Edge Support Important on an Adjustable Bed?

It can be especially useful.

An adjustable bed changes the position of the mattress as the head or foot sections rise. The mattress therefore needs to remain flexible enough to articulate with the base while continuing to provide comfortable support.

A lower-profile mattress with a supportive perimeter can provide a good combination of flexibility and stability. All three mattresses highlighted above are designed for compatibility with adjustable bases.

Frequently Asked Questions

What mattress has the best edge support?

Mattresses with reinforced perimeter construction generally provide better edge support. Hybrid mattresses with foam-encased coils, high-density memory foam mattresses and reinforced adjustable air mattresses can all provide strong edge support.

What does edge support mean in a mattress?

Edge support describes how well the sides of a mattress resist compression when someone sits or sleeps near the perimeter. Better edge support generally creates a more stable edge and increases the usable sleeping surface.

Are memory foam mattresses good for edge support?

They can be. A memory foam mattress with edge support typically uses higher-density support foam or other reinforcement to create greater stability around the perimeter.

Do hybrid mattresses have better edge support?

Many hybrid mattresses have excellent edge support because manufacturers can reinforce the perimeter surrounding the coil system. However, edge support varies by mattress, so construction is more important than the "hybrid" label alone.

Does edge support make a mattress last longer?

Strong perimeter construction can help reduce excessive compression along the sides, but overall mattress durability depends on the quality of the foams, coils, support core and other materials used throughout the mattress.
