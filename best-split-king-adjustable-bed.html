Short Answer

The best split king adjustable bed is the one that matches your needs. Pick a voice control base if you want hands free control, a wallhugger if you read or watch TV in bed, lumbar support if you have lower back pain, and a hi low base if getting in and out of bed is hard. If you do not need massage, do not pay for it.

What Is a Split King Adjustable Bed?

A split adjustable king bed is two Twin XL adjustable bases (about 38" x 80" each) set side by side to form a standard king. Each side has its own motors and remote. One person can raise their head to stop snoring while the other stays flat.

Split King Adjustable Bed FAQs

What is a split king adjustable bed?

A split king adjustable bed is two Twin XL adjustable bases placed side by side to make a king size bed. Each side has its own motor and remote, so each sleeper can raise their head or feet without moving the other person.

Do I need two mattresses for a split king adjustable bed frame?

Yes, a standard split king adjustable bed frame uses two Twin XL mattresses, one on each base. If you want one king mattress on top, look at a split top (split head) model where only the head section splits.

Can I use my existing bed frame or headboard with an adjustable split king bed?

Most adjustable split king bed frames can sit inside a standard king bed frame or stand alone on their own legs. Many also accept headboard brackets, so ask us about your frame before you order.

Is a split king adjustable bed bundle worth it?

A split king adjustable bed bundle pairs the bases with mattresses that are made to flex, so you avoid buying a mattress that fights the frame. It also means one order, one delivery and one point of contact for support.
