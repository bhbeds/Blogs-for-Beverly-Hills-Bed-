What Is an Adjustable Firmness Mattress?

By the Beverly Hills Bed Sleep Team  |  September 25, 2026  |  6 min read

Most mattresses lock you into one feel the day you buy them. If it turns out too soft, too firm, or your partner likes it differently, you are stuck. An adjustable firmness mattress solves that problem by letting you change the firmness of a mattress whenever you want, with the push of a button.

Short Answer

An adjustable firmness mattress is a bed with built-in air chambers that you inflate or deflate with a remote to make it firmer or softer. Dual chamber models give each side its own setting, so couples can each choose their ideal firmness on the same mattress.

How Does an Adjustable Firmness Mattress Work?

Inside the mattress are one or two air chambers, topped with comfort layers and a pillow top. A quiet pump connects to the chambers. When you raise the number on the remote, the pump adds air and the bed gets firmer. When you lower it, air is released and the bed gets softer.

Single Chamber vs. Dual Chamber

A single chamber mattress changes the firmness of the whole bed at once, which works well for one sleeper. A dual firmness mattress has two chambers, one per side, so it acts as a mattress with adjustable firmness on each side. This is also called a split firmness mattress.

What the Numbers on the Remote Mean

The remote shows a firmness number for each side. There is no "right" number. Start in the middle, sleep on it for a few nights, then move up or down in small steps until your hips and shoulders feel supported without pressure.

Benefits of an Adjustable Firmness Mattress

Couples Stop Compromising

The most common reason people buy a firmness adjustable mattress is a partner who sleeps differently. One person can set a softer side for shoulder relief while the other sets a firmer side for back support. This is why a dual chamber model is often the best adjustable firmness mattress for couples.

Support That Changes With You

Your needs shift with an injury, a change in weight, pregnancy, or simply age. With a traditional bed, that means buying a new mattress. With an adjustable firmness mattress, you just change the setting.

Easier to Find Your Ideal Firmness

Lying on a showroom bed for five minutes rarely tells you how it will feel after a full night. With mattress adjustable firmness, you can test settings at home and take the guesswork out of choosing.

Pairs Well With an Adjustable Base

Air mattresses flex easily, so they work with adjustable bases. Raise the head for reading or snoring, raise the feet for circulation, and still keep your preferred firmness.

Who Should Consider an Adjustable Firmness Mattress?

  • Couples who disagree on how firm the bed should be.
  • Side sleepers who need a softer surface for shoulders and hips.
  • Back and stomach sleepers who need firmer support to keep hips from sinking.
  • Combination sleepers who want to fine tune one setting that works in every position.
  • Anyone unsure of what firmness they actually prefer.

How to Choose the Best Adjustable Firmness Mattress

1. Start With Your Sleep Position

Side sleepers usually do best on the plush 14", back sleepers on the medium 12", and stomach sleepers on the firmer 10". You can still adjust each one from there.

2. Choose Dual Chambers if You Share the Bed

If you sleep with a partner, a mattress with adjustable firmness on each side is the feature that matters most.

3. Think About Your Base

Planning to add an adjustable base now or later? Choose a split king if you and your partner want to raise your sides independently. Your firmness settings stay the same no matter how the base is positioned.

Beverly Hills Bed Tip

When you first set up your mattress, change the number in small steps and give each setting two or three nights. Your body needs a little time to adjust to a new level of support.

Adjustable Firmness Mattress FAQs

How does an adjustable firmness mattress work?

Air chambers inside the mattress are filled or released by a quiet pump. More air makes the bed firmer and less air makes it softer.

Can each side of the mattress have a different firmness?

Yes. Dual chamber models let each sleeper set their own side with a separate remote setting.

Is an adjustable firmness mattress good for back pain?

It can help because you can fine tune support until your spine feels neutral. If you have a medical condition, check with your doctor about the best support level for you.

Does an adjustable firmness mattress work with an adjustable base?

Yes. Our air mattresses are designed to flex with adjustable bases, including split king setups.

Which thickness should I choose?

Pick the 14 inch for a plush feel, the 12 inch for a balanced medium feel, or the 10 inch for a firmer feel. All three still let you adjust firmness with the remote.
