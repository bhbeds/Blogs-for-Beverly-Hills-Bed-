Beverly Hills Bed 14" vs. Sleep Number ComfortNext™ Ultra: An Honest Head-to-Head

Beverly Hills Bed  |  May 2026  |  8 min read

Beverly Hills Bed (BHB) 14" Air Mattress with Adjustable Massaging Base is a direct-to-consumer adjustable air bed system with a thick pillow top and integrated adjustable base options. Sleep Number ComfortNext™ Ultra is a premium 14" adjustable air mattress from Sleep Number's newer ComfortNext collection (launched around 2026), emphasizing advanced cooling, pressure relief, and smart features.

Both use dual air chambers for per-side firmness adjustment (ideal for couples), but they differ significantly in design philosophy, included features, pricing, and extras. BHB positions itself as a high-value alternative to Sleep Number, often claiming similar adjustability at much lower cost.

Comfort & Support

Both: Excellent for couples due to independent sides. Good for back pain via customizable firmness and pressure relief. Air chambers contour well and distribute weight evenly.

BHB Advantages: Flippable internal topper adds extra customization (latex for responsive/cooling feel vs. memory foam for contouring). 50 fine settings + manual control lets you tweak instantly (e.g., firmer for sitting up). Pillow top feels plush/luxurious. Users replacing Sleep Number often report similar or better feel.

Sleep Number Advantages: Responsive Air for automatic micro-adjustments, micro coils for targeted support (hips/lower back/knees/shoulders), advanced layering for consistent pressure relief. Tri-Brid design aims for longer-lasting support.

Edge Support: Both strong; BHB emphasizes Power Edge design.

Cooling & Temperature Regulation

Sleep Number Ultra likely superior with significantly more cooling materials, micro coils for airflow, and overall design focus on heat dissipation. Good for hot sleepers.

BHB: Breathable pillow top, latex option (natural cooling via pin-core airflow), moisture barrier. Claims temperature-neutral but less quantified emphasis than Sleep Number.

Adjustability & Features

BHB: Massaging base (built-in), lumbar/pillow tilt options, zero gravity, programmable positions, voice control on some bases. Simpler remotes (no app dependency).

Sleep Number: App/smart features, sleep tracking/insights, potential foot warming on bases. Auto-adjust tech.

Noise: Both pumps quiet (brief operation); bases whisper-quiet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can couples with different firmness preferences use either of these mattresses comfortably?
Yes — both the BHB 14" and the Sleep Number ComfortNext™ Ultra feature independent dual air chambers, meaning each person can dial in their own firmness setting without affecting the other side. This makes either mattress an excellent choice for couples. BHB uses dual wireless remotes with digital displays; Sleep Number uses its app and remote system. Each side is completely independent from head to toe.
How long does it take to receive a Beverly Hills Bed mattress, and why?
BHB mattresses are made-to-order, typically taking 2–4 weeks from purchase to delivery. Because each mattress is built to order — including the option for custom sizes — production lead time is longer than off-the-shelf brands. This also ensures you receive a freshly made product rather than one sitting in a warehouse.
What is the flippable internal topper on the BHB, and why does it matter?
The BHB 14" includes a 3" internal topper that can be flipped to either side: one side is 1.5" latex (cooler and more responsive) and the other is 1.5" Memory-Cell foam (softer and more contouring). This allows you to effectively change the feel of your mattress without buying a new one — a unique advantage over Sleep Number's fixed Tri-Brid layer construction.
Does the Beverly Hills Bed require an app or smartphone to operate?
No. BHB operates entirely through its dual wireless remotes with digital displays — no app required. This is a deliberate design choice for users who prefer simple, direct control. Sleep Number, by contrast, relies heavily on its smartphone app for fine-tuning firmness, accessing sleep data, and managing Responsive Air auto-adjustments.
Is the adjustable base included with the Beverly Hills Bed, or is it extra like Sleep Number's?
With BHB, the bundle price includes both the mattress and a premium adjustable massaging base — starting with the 400 series, which includes wireless remotes, memory positions, programmable massage with timers, under-bed lighting, and zero-clearance design. Sleep Number bases (foundations and FlexFit adjustable models) are sold separately and can add $500–$4,000+ to the total cost.
Which mattress is better for hot sleepers?
Sleep Number ComfortNext™ Ultra has a meaningful edge for hot sleepers, featuring 175% more cooling materials compared to Sleep Number's base models, high-density micro coils that promote airflow throughout the mattress, and an overall design philosophy centered on heat dissipation. BHB offers a breathable pillow top and a latex topper option (natural latex is inherently cooler than memory foam), but its cooling claims are less quantified than Sleep Number's.
How noisy are the pumps on each mattress?
Both mattresses are designed with quiet pumps that only operate briefly when adjusting firmness — they are not continuously running. BHB's in-rail pump is built into the mattress frame itself (no hoses on the floor), which contributes to a cleaner setup and reportedly less mechanical noise. Sleep Number's pump is typically mounted under the bed. Both are considered whisper-quiet in daily use, as are their respective adjustable bases.
Can the Beverly Hills Bed be used with my existing bed frame or base?
BHB mattresses can be used with compatible bed frames and platforms. The zero-clearance design of BHB's included bases means they sit flat on the floor without requiring a traditional frame. If you already own an adjustable base, contact BHB directly to confirm compatibility, especially given the in-rail pump design. Custom size options also make BHB flexible for non-standard frame situations.
Is either mattress covered by HSA or FSA funds?
Sleep Number explicitly offers HSA/FSA eligibility for qualifying purchases. BHB does not prominently advertise HSA/FSA compatibility — if this is important to you, confirm directly with BHB before purchasing. In general, adjustable beds used for medically documented conditions can sometimes qualify; your HSA/FSA administrator has the final say.
What is the warranty difference, and why does it matter?
BHB offers a 25-year prorated mattress warranty (with 3 full years of non-prorated coverage), compared to Sleep Number's 10–15 year limited warranty (with 2 full years). A longer warranty signals confidence in durability and provides long-term protection for a significant investment. BHB's base warranty ranges from 10–20 years depending on the model. Always read the full warranty terms — “prorated” means your coverage value decreases over time.
I currently own a Sleep Number. Is switching to BHB worth it?
Many BHB customers are former Sleep Number owners, and reviews frequently cite a similar or better comfort experience at a significantly lower cost — particularly when factoring in the included adjustable base. The flippable topper, in-rail pump, and bundled massage features are upgrades over comparable Sleep Number configurations. The primary trade-offs are the 2–4 week lead time (vs. faster delivery), less app-based smart tech, and a newer brand with a smaller online review volume than Sleep Number's long-established ecosystem.
