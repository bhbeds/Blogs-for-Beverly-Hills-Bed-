Helix markets the Midnight Luxe as its best selling mattress, and it's a common search when shoppers compare premium King beds. Below is a straight, no fluff comparison of the Helix Midnight Luxe and the Beverly Hills Bed 12" Air Mattress, both configured as a King mattress plus adjustable massage base bundle.

Short Answer

The Beverly Hills Bed 12" Air Mattress King bundle (mattress plus adjustable massage base) runs about $3,499, compared to about $3,948 for the equivalent Helix Midnight Luxe King bundle at the time of writing. Beverly Hills Bed also gives you 50 individual air firmness settings and multiple adjustable base options with lumbar support, voice control, pillow tilt, and hi-low positioning, versus one fixed feel and one base option from Helix. Beverly Hills Bed backs the mattress with a 25 year warranty.

Price: Which King Bundle Costs Less

At the time of writing, the Helix Midnight Luxe King mattress with an adjustable massage base totals $3,948.00 after discount pricing. The same style bundle, a King 12" air mattress with an adjustable massage base from Beverly Hills Bed, totals $3,499.00. That is a difference of $449 in Beverly Hills Bed's favor, before you factor in the extra firmness control and base features described below.

What You're Actually Paying For

Helix's price covers a foam hybrid mattress with a single, factory set comfort level and one adjustable base choice. Beverly Hills Bed's price covers a 12" air mattress with 50 individually adjustable firmness settings, paired with a choice of adjustable bases that include lumbar support, voice control, pillow tilt, and hi-low height adjustment.

Firmness: 50 Air Settings vs One Fixed Feel

The core difference between these two mattresses is how firmness works. The Helix Midnight Luxe ships with one comfort level built into the foam layers, so the feel you order is the feel you keep. The Beverly Hills Bed 12" Air Mattress uses air chambers with 50 separate settings, so either side of the bed can be adjusted from soft to firm at any time, without buying a new mattress.

Adjustable Base Features Compared

Lumbar Support, Voice Control, Pillow Tilt, and Hi-Low Positioning

Helix offers one adjustable base option to pair with the Midnight Luxe. Beverly Hills Bed offers a wider range of adjustable bases, including features Helix does not offer at all: lumbar support for lower back pain, voice control for hands free adjustments, pillow tilt for reading or watching TV in bed, and hi-low height adjustment for easier entry and exit.

Warranty Comparison

Beverly Hills Bed backs the 12" air mattress with a 25 year warranty, detailed on our warranty page. Helix publishes its own warranty terms for the Midnight Luxe on its Helix mattress warranty page, worth reading in full before you buy so you know exactly what is and is not covered.

Which King Mattress Should You Choose

If you want a well known foam hybrid brand and you are confident one fixed firmness will suit you and any partner for years, the Helix Midnight Luxe is a straightforward option. If you want to control firmness yourself, want an adjustable base with more features, want a longer published warranty, and want to spend less doing it, the Beverly Hills Bed 12" Air Mattress bundle is the stronger buy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Helix Midnight Luxe or the Beverly Hills Bed 12" Air Mattress cheaper for a King bundle?

The Beverly Hills Bed King bundle, mattress plus adjustable massage base, is $3,499, about $449 less than the equivalent $3,948 Helix Midnight Luxe bundle at the time of writing. Prices on both sides can change with promotions, so check current pricing before you buy.

Can I change the firmness of the Helix Midnight Luxe after I buy it?

No, the Midnight Luxe has one fixed comfort level built into its foam layers, so the feel does not change after purchase. The Beverly Hills Bed 12" Air Mattress can be adjusted through 50 separate air settings any time.

Does Helix offer an adjustable base with lumbar support or voice control?

Helix offers one adjustable base option for the Midnight Luxe, and it does not include lumbar support, voice control, pillow tilt, or hi-low height adjustment. Beverly Hills Bed offers multiple adjustable base choices that include all four of those features.

How long is the warranty on each mattress?

Beverly Hills Bed covers the 12" air mattress with a 25 year warranty, listed on our warranty page. Helix publishes its own warranty terms for the Midnight Luxe on its warranty page, so review it directly for coverage details.

Is an air mattress as durable as a foam hybrid mattress like the Helix Midnight Luxe?

Beverly Hills Bed's 12" air mattress is built for daily use and is backed by a 25 year warranty, which is a strong durability signal on its own. Unlike a fixed foam mattress, it also lets you keep adjusting firmness as your needs change instead of replacing the mattress.
