Adjustable Bed Frame Features Worth Paying For

The best adjustable bed frame and mattress combo is the one with features you will use every night. These are the bases we carry and who each one helps:

  • Lumbar back support: a separate adjustment that lifts the lower back, useful for back pain and long reading sessions.
  • Hi-low bases for seniors: the whole bed raises and lowers, which makes getting in and out easier and safer.
  • Voice control: move the bed hands free, helpful for limited mobility or a lost remote.
  • Massage: head and foot zones with timer settings to relax before sleep.
  • Pillow tilt: angles your head forward so you can read or watch TV without stacking pillows.
  • Zero gravity and anti snore presets: one touch positions that take pressure off the spine or slightly raise the head.

Split Queen and Split Cal King: Sizes for Couples

A split size uses two mattresses on two bases (or one dual base) so each side moves on its own. One partner can sit up to read while the other stays flat.

Split king is common, but many bedrooms fit a queen better, and many retailers do not carry split queen or split cal king. We do, so you can get independent sides without changing your bed size. Split queen is two 30" x 80" halves, and split cal king is two halves of a 72" x 84" bed.

King and Queen Size Bed and Mattress Combo Deals: What to Check

When comparing king size bed and mattress combo deals or queen size bed and mattress combo deals, the lowest price is not always the best value. Check these before you buy:

  • Mattress compatibility: foam, air, and latex flex well; a traditional innerspring with a stiff border often does not.
  • Weight capacity: look for the total for both sleepers plus the mattress.
  • Warranty on the base: motors and frames should be covered for years, not months.
  • Leg height and zero clearance: make sure the base fits your existing frame or platform.
  • Split options: confirm the size you want is truly split if both sleepers want to adjust separately.

Adjustable Bed and Mattress Combo FAQs

What is the best adjustable bed and mattress combo?

The best combo depends on your budget and how much control you want over comfort. Memory foam is best for value, air is best for couples with different firmness needs, and latex is best for long term flexibility.

What is the cheapest bed frame and mattress combo that adjusts?

A memory foam mattress paired with an entry level adjustable base is usually the lowest cost option. Our 10" memory foam combo with the Classic 300 Series base still includes massage and wireless remotes.

Can I use any mattress on an adjustable base?

Memory foam, air, and latex mattresses work best because they bend without damage. Many traditional innerspring mattresses are too stiff and can wear out quickly on an adjustable base.

Is a split queen adjustable bed worth it?

Yes, if you and your partner want different positions or firmness on each side. A split queen gives you that independence without moving up to a king size bed.

Which adjustable base is best for seniors?

A hi-low base is often the best choice because it raises the bed for easier entry and exit. Adding voice control makes it even easier to use day to day.
