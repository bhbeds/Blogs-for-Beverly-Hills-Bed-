What's the Coolest Mattress for Hot Sleepers? Air and Latex Lead the Way

By the Beverly Hills Bed Sleep Team  |  Updated October 13, 2026  |  6 min read

If you wake up sweaty, kick the covers off at 2 a.m., or flip your pillow looking for the cool side, your mattress may be part of the problem. Some materials hold onto body heat all night. Others let it escape. This guide explains what mattress sleeps the coolest, why, and how to pick the coolest mattress to sleep on for your body.

Short Answer

The coolest mattress for hot sleepers is an air mattress or a latex mattress. Air chambers have no dense foam to absorb heat, and latex is naturally open-celled and breathable, so both stay close to room temperature through the night. Traditional memory foam is the most likely to sleep hot because it softens with body heat and hugs the body, which limits airflow.

Which Mattress Materials Sleep the Coolest Overnight?

How hot a mattress feels comes down to two things: how much heat the material absorbs and how easily air can move through it. Here is how the most common materials compare, ranked from coolest to warmest.

Air mattresses: the coolest sleeping mattress

Our air mattresses support you with adjustable air chambers instead of a thick block of foam. Air does not soak up and store body heat the way dense foam does, so the surface stays cool even on warm nights. You also control the firmness, so you sit on top of the bed instead of sinking in, which keeps more of your body exposed to circulating air.

Latex mattresses: cool, breathable, and responsive

Latex has an open-cell structure with natural pinholes that let air pass through. It pushes back gently instead of molding tightly around you, so heat is not trapped against your skin. Many hot sleepers who try latex call it the coolest foam mattress they have owned.

Memory foam: comfortable, but it sometimes gets hot

Memory foam reacts to body heat. As it warms, it softens and contours closely to your shape, and that close hug reduces airflow around your body. Not every memory foam bed sleeps hot, and gel or copper infusions help, but if you already run warm, memory foam is the material most likely to leave you sweating. Shoppers searching for the coolest memory foam mattress are often better served by latex, which gives a similar pressure relief without the heat.

How to Choose the Coolest Mattress to Sleep On

  • Start with the core material. Air chambers and latex keep heat moving. Dense memory foam holds it.
  • Get the firmness right. A bed that is too soft lets you sink in, which wraps more of your body and adds heat.
  • Keep the layers breathable. A thin, breathable cover and protector keep a cool mattress cool.
  • Think about your partner. Two bodies make more heat, and an air mattress lets each side set its own firmness.
Beverly Hills Bed Tip

A cooling mattress topper or mattress pad can help a warm bed for a while, but it cannot change a core that traps heat. If you are already shopping for a coolest mattress topper, it may be time to look at an air or latex mattress instead.

Coolest Mattress for Hot Sleepers FAQs

What is the coolest mattress to sleep on?

Air mattresses and natural latex mattresses are the coolest mattresses to sleep on because both let air move freely and do not trap body heat. Traditional memory foam is usually the warmest option.

Does memory foam sleep hot?

Memory foam can sleep hot because it softens with body heat and wraps closely around you, which limits airflow. Gel infusions help a little, but most hot sleepers still feel cooler on air or latex.

Which mattress materials sleep the coolest?

Air chambers and latex sleep the coolest, followed by innerspring and hybrid designs. Dense, slow response memory foam holds the most heat.

Will a cooling mattress topper or pad fix a hot mattress?

A latex topper or breathable mattress pad can take the edge off a warm bed for a while. It cannot fully fix a mattress core that traps heat, so replacing the mattress is the lasting solution.

What is the coolest mattress protector?

The coolest mattress protector is a thin, breathable one made from cotton, bamboo, or Tencel with a waterproof membrane that still lets air through. Avoid thick vinyl or plastic protectors because they block airflow and hold heat.

The Bottom Line on the Coolest Mattress

If you sleep hot, the material inside your mattress matters more than any cooling gimmick on top of it. Air mattresses and latex mattresses do not get hot the way memory foam sometimes does, which makes them the coolest mattress choices for hot sleepers. Pick the one that matches the feel you like, and you can stop fighting the heat every night.
