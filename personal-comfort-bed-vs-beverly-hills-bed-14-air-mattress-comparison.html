Beverly Hills Bed — 14" Air Adjustable Mattress

Beverly Hills Bed's 14" air mattress is built to compare directly with the Sleep Number Comfort Next Ultra, with 50 individual firmness increments per side and dual air chambers so each sleeper controls their own feel. A flippable comfort layer sits above the air bladders, giving you a second firmness option without buying a new mattress. Unlike most air beds and adjustable air mattresses on the market, the pumps are fully encased inside the mattress itself — nothing sits on the floor to snag, collect dust, or get chewed on by the family dog.

Which Adjustable Air Bed Should You Buy?

Best overall value: If you want the tallest profile, in-mattress pumps, and the lowest total price on a fully equipped split king, the Beverly Hills Bed 14" air mattress with massaging adjustable base is the clear pick. At $4,880 for the complete bundle, it undercuts the Personal Comfort Rejuvenate by nearly $3,000 while still delivering 50-step firmness control on each side.

Best for hot sleepers who want app control: If a full 25-year non-prorated warranty and free cooling matter more to you than price, Personal Comfort's Rejuvenate bundle is worth the premium. Just know you're paying almost double for those extras, and you'll be sleeping on a mattress that's an inch shorter with pumps sitting outside the mattress shell rather than tucked away inside it.

Budget-conscious couples and anyone furnishing a guest room, primary bedroom, or RV bunk will likely find the Beverly Hills Bed bundle hard to beat — it's the rare case where the more affordable adjustable air mattress also happens to have the taller, more streamlined build. Personal Comfort still makes sense if cooling and a fully non-prorated warranty are non-negotiable for you.

Whichever direction you lean, both mattresses beat what you'd pay for a comparable Sleep Number setup — but only one of them keeps the pumps out of sight and the bundle price under $5,000. Is Beverly Hills Bed's 14" air mattress really cheaper than Personal Comfort?

Yes. As of 2026, the Beverly Hills Bed split king + massaging adjustable base bundle is $4,880, compared to $7,799 for Personal Comfort's Rejuvenate bundle — a difference of $2,919.

Which mattress is taller, the Rejuvenate or the Beverly Hills Bed 14"?

Beverly Hills Bed's air mattress measures 14", one inch taller than the Personal Comfort Rejuvenate's 13" profile.

Does the Beverly Hills Bed air mattress have a smartphone app?

The mattress firmness is remote-controlled rather than app-controlled. The included massaging adjustable base offers wall-hugger, pillow tilt, lumbar support, and app-enabled positioning.

Why does Personal Comfort cost more for a comparable bundle?

Personal Comfort's higher price reflects its full (non-prorated) 25-year warranty and the free BedJet cooling system included with adjustable base bundles — both genuine value-adds, just at a premium.

Where are the air pumps located on each mattress?

Beverly Hills Bed encases both air pumps fully inside the 14" mattress, so nothing sits on the floor. Personal Comfort's pumps are not encased inside the mattress.
