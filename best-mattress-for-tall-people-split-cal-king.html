Best Bed and Mattress for Tall People: Sizes & Adjustable Bed Options

Beverly Hills Bed  |  September 14, 2026  |  7 min read

If your feet hang off the end of the bed or your toes press against the footboard, you've outgrown your mattress, not the other way around. Finding the right mattress for tall people comes down to one number: length. Here's how to size up correctly, including why a Cal King or Split Cal King mattress is the go-to upgrade for taller sleepers and couples.

Quick Answer

The best mattress for tall people is a California King or Split California King, since both measure 84 inches long, four inches longer than a Queen or King. A Split Cal King mattress is two 36 by 84 inch halves on one frame, so each sleeper can pair it with their own adjustable base. Sleepers over 6'5" should also look for a tall, extra-thick mattress (12 to 14 inches) paired with an adjustable bed for full head-to-foot support.

How Tall Is Too Tall for a Standard Mattress?

A standard Queen or King mattress is 80 inches long. That's enough room for most adults, but once you're taller than about 6'2", your feet start reaching the edge of the bed. Add a pillow, a partner's cold feet, or a habit of curling up diagonally, and even 80 inches feels short.

This is the gap that a tall mattress or big and tall mattress is built to close. Instead of squeezing into a standard length, taller sleepers move up to a size with four extra inches of legroom: the California King.

Cal King vs. Split Cal King Mattress: What's the Difference?

What Is a Split California King Mattress?

A split cal king mattress is two separate 36 by 84 inch mattresses placed side by side on one bed frame to create a full 72 by 84 inch sleep surface. Cover it with a standard Cal King fitted sheet and you'd never know it was two mattresses. The real advantage shows up underneath: each half can sit on its own adjustable base, so one partner can raise their head for reading while the other stays flat.

Split King vs. Cal King for Tall Sleepers

Don't confuse a split cal king with a split king. A split king is two Twin XL mattresses, each 80 inches long. A split cal king uses two longer mattresses at 84 inches long, the same length as a regular Cal King. For sleepers over 6'5", that extra 4 inches is exactly what a standard split king is missing.

Beverly Hills Bed Tip

If you and your partner are both tall, or one of you is significantly taller, a split cal king mattress on two adjustable bases lets each side match a different mattress length preference, firmness, and incline, without either of you compromising.

How Tall Is a Mattress? Thickness vs. Length Explained

"How tall is a mattress" is a different question from "how long is a mattress." Length is the head-to-foot measurement covered above. Thickness (sometimes called mattress height) is how far the mattress rises off the frame, and it usually runs 8 to 14 inches depending on the number of foam, coil, and pillow-top layers.

Taller, heavier sleepers generally do better on a thicker mattress, 12 inches or more, because it has room for deeper support layers that won't bottom out under extra height and weight. An extra tall mattress in the 12 to 14 inch range also pairs better with an adjustable base, since there's enough material to flex smoothly at the head and foot without losing support.

How to Choose the Right Tall Mattress for You

Match Mattress Length to Your Height

As a rule of thumb, your mattress should be at least 6 to 8 inches longer than you are tall. Anyone over 6'2" should skip Queen and King and go straight to a California King or Split California King for real legroom.

Pick a Supportive, Adjustable Firmness

Taller bodies distribute weight differently, so a one-size firmness rarely fits both partners. An adjustable air mattress lets each sleeper set their own support level without buying two separate beds.

Add an Adjustable Base for Full-Length Support

An adjustable base with head and foot articulation gives tall sleepers extra room to stretch out, elevate their feet, or sit up to read, all without leaving the bed. Paired with a Split Cal King mattress, each side of the bed moves independently.
