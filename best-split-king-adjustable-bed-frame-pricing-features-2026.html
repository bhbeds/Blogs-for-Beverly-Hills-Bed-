What Is a Split King Adjustable Bed Frame?

A split king adjustable bed frame pairs two independently powered adjustable bases — each sized as a twin XL — under a single king mattress setup (typically two twin XL mattresses or a specialty split-king mattress). Each side has its own remote, motor, and often its own app or voice control, so incline angle, massage intensity, and preset positions are fully personalized. It's the most popular configuration for couples upgrading to an adjustable bed frame for split king mattresses because neither partner has to compromise on comfort.

Key Features to Look For

  • Wall-hugger design: Keeps the mattress near your nightstand as the base reclines, instead of sliding the whole bed forward.
  • Independent controls: Each side of a split king adjustable bed frame should have its own remote or app profile.
  • Massage zones: Look for adjustable intensity and targeted zones (lumbar, calves) rather than a single vibration setting.
  • Voice control: Increasingly common on premium models — useful for hands-free adjustments at night.
  • Weight capacity and warranty: Confirm the base supports your mattress type and check motor warranty length before buying.

Split King vs. Split California King Adjustable Bed Frames

A standard split king adjustable bed frame uses two 38" x 80" twin XL bases. A split California king adjustable bed frame swaps in two 36" x 84" bases instead — four inches longer, three inches narrower. Taller sleepers (generally 6'2" and up) often prefer the split cal king adjustable bed frame for the extra legroom, while standard split king remains the more common and widely available choice. Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a split king and a split California king adjustable bed frame?

A split king uses two 38" x 80" twin XL bases, while a split California king uses two 36" x 84" bases — four inches longer and three inches narrower. Taller sleepers typically prefer the split cal king adjustable bed frame for the added legroom.

How much does a split king adjustable bed frame cost?

Prices range from around $899 for value models to $2,800+ for premium options with voice control, lumbar support, and pillow tilt. Mid-range models with massage and zero gravity typically fall between $1,300 and $2,000.

Can I use my existing headboard with a split king adjustable base?

Many split king adjustable bed frames with headboard brackets are compatible with standard headboards, but frame height and bracket spacing vary by model. Check the specific base's compatibility details or contact our team before purchasing.

Do split king adjustable beds require special mattresses?

You'll need a mattress flexible enough to bend at the base's hinge points — most memory foam, latex, and hybrid mattresses work well. We offer adjustable split king bed frame and mattress bundles specifically matched for smooth, quiet flexing.

Are split king adjustable bases good for couples with different sleep needs?

Yes — that's their main advantage. Each side operates independently, so one partner can elevate for reading, snoring relief, or acid reflux while the other stays flat, all on the same bed.
