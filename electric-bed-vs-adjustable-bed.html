Electric Bed vs Adjustable Bed: What's the Difference?

Beverly Hills Bed  |  August 4, 2026  |  3 min read

Short answer: there is virtually no difference between an electric bed and an adjustable bed. Both terms describe a bed base powered by electric motors that lets you raise and lower the head and foot for personalized comfort. The biggest difference is simply the terminology manufacturers and retailers use.

Are Electric Beds and Adjustable Beds the Same?

Yes. In today's bedding industry, the terms electric bed, electric adjustable bed, and adjustable bed are used interchangeably. They all refer to a motorized bed base that lets you change your sleeping or lounging position using a wired or wireless remote, a smartphone app, or even voice control on select models. Whether a product is labeled an electric bed or an adjustable bed, you're looking at the same type of sleep system.

Why Do Companies Use Different Names?

The wording usually comes down to marketing rather than product differences. "Electric bed" emphasizes that the bed is powered by electric motors, "adjustable bed" focuses on the ability to adjust the head and foot sections, and "electric adjustable bed" combines both. Most manufacturers now prefer "adjustable bed," while many shoppers still search "electric beds" — especially for home-use models.

How Does It Work?

Electric adjustable beds use one or more quiet motors mounted beneath the sleep surface that move different sections independently. Common features include:

  • Independent head and foot adjustment
  • Zero Gravity preset and Anti-Snore position
  • Wireless remote and programmable memory positions
  • Massage functions and under-bed lighting
  • USB charging ports and voice control
  • Wallhugger technology and split configurations for couples

Premium models may also include lumbar support, pillow tilt, and Bluetooth smartphone controls.

Benefits of an Electric Bed

Beyond convenience, an electric adjustable bed offers personalized comfort for reading, watching TV, or relaxing after a long day, and better sleep positioning without stacking pillows. Split King and Split Queen models let couples control their own side independently, and premium features like massage and Zero Gravity turn an ordinary bedroom into a luxury sleep space.

Mattress and Size Compatibility

Not every mattress works on an adjustable base. Memory foam, latex, hybrid mattresses designed for adjustable bases, and adjustable air mattresses flex easily without damaging internal materials. Electric adjustable beds are available in Twin XL, Full, Queen, Split Queen, King, Split King, and Split Cal King — split sizes let each sleeper adjust their side independently.

Final Thought

When comparing an electric bed vs adjustable bed, they're essentially the same product — the difference is terminology, not technology. Focus less on the name and more on the features that matter to you: massage, Zero Gravity, wallhugger technology, split sleeping options, wireless controls, and mattress compatibility.

FAQ

Is an electric bed the same as an adjustable bed?

Yes. Both terms refer to a motorized bed base that lets you adjust the head and foot positions using electric motors.

Why are they called electric beds?

The name comes from the electric motors that power the bed's adjustable movements.

Are electric beds only for seniors?

No. Electric beds are popular with adults of all ages who want more comfort for reading, watching TV, relaxing, or sleeping.

Can couples use electric adjustable beds?

Yes. Split King and Split Queen models let each sleeper adjust their own side independently.

Do electric beds require a special mattress?

Most memory foam, latex, hybrid, and adjustable air mattresses are compatible. Always verify mattress compatibility before purchasing.
