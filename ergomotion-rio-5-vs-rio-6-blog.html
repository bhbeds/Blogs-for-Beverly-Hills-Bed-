Ergomotion Rio 5 vs Ergomotion Rio 6: Which Adjustable Base Should You Buy?

By the Beverly Hills Bed Sleep Team · Updated November 9, 2026

The Ergomotion Rio 5 and Rio 6 share the same core features: independent head and foot motion, Zero-G, anti-snore, massage, lumbar support, head tilt, wall-hugger design, under-bed lighting, and USB-A and USB-C charging. The real difference is build and price. The Rio 5 is a lower-profile base that fits inside your existing bed frame, while the Rio 6 is a furniture-style, upholstered base built to stand on its own.

We sell both models, and this guide uses the specs from our product pages and Ergomotion's published documents so you can pick the right one in a few minutes.

Short Answer

Choose the Ergomotion Rio 5 if you want to keep your current bed frame, need a Full size, or want the lower price (Queen $1,489).

Choose the Ergomotion Rio 6 if you want a stand-alone, upholstered base with a taller 7.5-inch deck and wood legs that looks like finished furniture (Queen $1,749).

Both have a 750 lb weight capacity, Zero-G, anti-snore, massage, lumbar, head tilt, and a 10-year limited warranty.

Key Differences Between the Ergomotion Rio 5 and Rio 6

1. Design and how it sits in your bedroom

The Rio 5 has a 5.1-inch deck and zero-clearance option, so it drops inside a platform bed, a decorative frame, or straight onto the floor. The Rio 6 is upholstered with a deck-on-deck build and 8-inch tapered wood legs, so it looks finished on its own and does not need a separate frame.

2. Price

The Rio 5 costs less in every size except Twin XL, where both are $1,399. The gap is $260 in Queen, $100 in King, and $400 in Split King.

3. Comfort features

Both have Zero-G, anti-snore, massage, lumbar, and head tilt. The Rio 6 adds massage timers (10, 20, 30 minutes) and an edge-to-edge lumbar that stops where you want it, while the Rio 5 offers three massage intensities and a 15-button remote with the ErgoSync app.

4. Size options

The Rio 5 comes in a Full size; the Rio 6 does not. If you have a Full mattress, the Rio 5 is your only option between these two.

5. Moving and setup

The Rio 5 folds for delivery and a Queen weighs about 172 lb. The Rio 6 Queen weighs about 235.9 lb, so plan on two people for setup.

Which Ergomotion Base Is Right for You?

Pick the Rio 5 if: you already own a bed frame you like, you need a Full size, you want app control, or you want the best price for a full-featured base.

Pick the Rio 6 if: you want the base to be the bed frame, you prefer an upholstered look with wood legs, you want massage timers, or you like a taller deck that is easier to get in and out of.

Ergomotion Rio 5 vs Rio 6 FAQs

What is the main difference between the Ergomotion Rio 5 and Rio 6?

The Rio 5 is a low-profile base made to sit inside an existing bed frame, while the Rio 6 is an upholstered, furniture-style base with wood legs that stands on its own. Both share Zero-G, anti-snore, massage, lumbar, and head tilt.

Is the Ergomotion Rio 6 worth the extra cost?

It is worth it if you want the base to replace your bed frame and prefer an upholstered look. If you plan to keep your current frame, the Rio 5 gives you nearly the same features for less.

What is the weight capacity of the Rio 5 and Rio 6?

Both bases are rated for 750 lb. That limit covers the mattress and sleepers combined.

Can I use the Ergomotion Rio 5 or Rio 6 with my existing bed frame?

Yes, both have a zero-clearance option, but the Rio 5's 5.1-inch deck makes it the better fit inside most frames. The Rio 6 is designed to be used without a frame.

What warranty comes with the Ergomotion Rio 5 and Rio 6?

Both come with a 10-year limited warranty covering the frame, motors, and electrical components. Full terms are listed on each product page.
